About M/S Pragati Pharmaceuticals

M/S Pragati Pharmaceuticals is a pharmaceutical wholesale distribution business based in Vijayapur, Karnataka. We supply pharmaceutical medicines, healthcare products, surgical consumables and hospital essentials to pharmacies, hospitals and other healthcare customers, with dependable service, competitive wholesale pricing and responsive support.
